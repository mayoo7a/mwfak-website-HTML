Brand
Identity

A full visual system your customers recognise everywhere.

Start a project

A brand identity is the set of visual decisions that makes a business easy to recognise and easy to trust. It covers your logo, your colours, your typefaces and the rules for using them, so everything you put out looks like it came from the same place.

I start with questions. Who are you serving, what do you want to sound like, and what makes you different from the other options your customers are looking at? The answers shape every choice that follows, so the identity fits your business instead of following a trend.

What you get

Research and positioning
A short brief that sums up your audience, your competitors and your voice. We agree on it before any design starts.
Logo and variations
Your main logo plus horizontal, stacked and icon-only versions, each in full colour, black and white.
Colour palette
Main and supporting colours with exact values for print and screen.
Typography
Typefaces for headings and body text, chosen to work in Arabic, English or both.
Brand guidelines
A document that shows how to use everything: the space around the logo, the smallest sizes, colour rules and what to avoid.
Applications
Examples of the identity on real items such as social posts, stationery, signs and merchandise.

Who it is for

New businesses that need a strong start, and established ones whose look no longer matches who they have become. It also suits teams whose materials come from several people and have started to look inconsistent.

How it works

  1. DiscoveryWe talk through your business, your audience and your goals.
  2. DirectionI present a clear visual direction and we choose the way forward together.
  3. DesignI build the full system and apply it to real examples.
  4. Hand-offYou receive the files and the guidelines, ready to use.

Logo
Design

One mark to carry your whole brand.

Start a project

A logo is the one piece of your brand that appears everywhere: on a phone screen, a shop sign, a shipping box and a stamp. It has to stay clear and recognisable at every size, so I draw it carefully and test it small and large before I show it to you.

I design Arabic, English and bilingual logos. When a logo uses both scripts, I balance them so neither one looks like an add-on. You bring the story of your business, and I turn it into a single mark that is simple, distinctive and easy to remember.

What you get

Concept exploration
Sketches and early ideas, so you can see how I reached the final direction.
Final logo
The chosen mark, refined curve by curve and letter by letter.
Language versions
Arabic, English or bilingual layouts, as your business needs.
Colour versions
Full colour, black, white, and a small icon version for places like social media profiles.
Usage rules
The clear space around the logo, the smallest size it works at, and examples of what not to do.
Final files
Vector files for print and image files for screens and social media.

Who it is for

Start-ups choosing their first logo, businesses replacing an old or unclear one, and brands going bilingual that need both scripts to look equally strong.

How it works

  1. BriefYou tell me about the business, the audience and the feeling the logo should give.
  2. SketchesI explore several directions and show you the strongest ones.
  3. RefinementI develop the chosen idea and test it at different sizes and on light and dark backgrounds.
  4. DeliveryYou receive the final logo in every version and every file you need.

UI/UX
Design

Flows people finish instead of abandoning.

Start a project

Good UI/UX design makes a website or an app easy to use without anyone having to think about it. When users get lost, they do not blame themselves, they leave. I look at how real people tap, scroll and decide, and I design the flow so they can finish what they came to do.

I design for every screen size, from a small phone to a large monitor, in both Arabic (right to left) and English. The result is a clear set of screens that looks good and that a developer can build from without guessing.

What you get

Research and goals
What your users need, what your business needs and where the two meet.
User flows
A map of the steps people take, so unnecessary steps can be removed before anything is designed.
Wireframes
Simple layouts that settle structure and content before colour and style.
Final screens
Polished designs for web and mobile, using your brand colours, type and style.
Right to left layouts
Arabic versions designed properly, with the layout mirrored instead of only translated.
Developer hand-off
Organised design files with sizes, spacing and assets, so the finished build matches the design.

Who it is for

Businesses launching a website or an app, teams whose current product confuses users, and companies adding Arabic to an English product or English to an Arabic one.

How it works

  1. UnderstandWe go through your users, your goals and what is not working today.
  2. MapI lay out the steps people take and decide what stays and what goes.
  3. DesignI move from wireframes to finished screens for every screen size.
  4. Hand-offYour developer receives organised files and clear specifications.

Packaging
& Print

A product that wins in its first two seconds.

Start a project

On a shelf, your package has about two seconds to be noticed. I design packaging, labels and printed pieces that stand out, tell people what the product is straight away, and still look good in a photo when someone unboxes it at home.

I also design printed material that has to be read, such as brochures, menus, catalogues and editorial layouts. Every file is prepared with printing in mind, so what you approve on screen is what comes off the press.

What you get

Packaging design
Boxes, bags, sleeves and jars, designed around the real shape and size of your product.
Label design
Labels with a clear order of information, so the product name, key details and required text are easy to find.
Print layouts
Brochures, menus, catalogues and other printed pieces, single page or many pages.
Print-ready files
Files prepared for your printer with the correct sizes, bleed and colour setup.
Mockups
Realistic previews of the finished product, useful for approvals, presentations and your online shop.

Who it is for

Product brands of any size, makers launching a first product, and businesses refreshing packaging that no longer reflects the quality inside.

How it works

  1. BriefYou tell me about the product, the audience and where it will be sold.
  2. ConceptsI present packaging directions and we pick one to develop.
  3. Design and mockupsI finish the design and show it on realistic mockups.
  4. Print preparationI prepare the files for your printer and check them before they go.
